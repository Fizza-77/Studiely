4
Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary for educational functionality, app improvement, and legal compliance. We do not use personal data for advertising or behavioral targeting.

4.1  Account & Identifiers
  • Email address
  • Firebase UID (unique identifier)
  • User role (Student, Parent, Teacher, Institution, Administrator)
4.2  Educational Content & Usage Data
  • Study notes, summaries, and generated materials
  • Quiz questions, answers, and results
  • Curriculum selections, subject/topic selections
  • Difficulty preferences and study modes
  • Learning progress and performance metrics
  • Quiz scores, completion data, and metadata
  • User preferences and settings
4.3  Analytics Data (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Screen views and navigation patterns
  • Feature usage and engagement metrics
  • In-app search topics (subjects/themes only)
  • Approximate location (country/region only)

We do not collect precise location data.

4.4  Technical & Device Data (via Firebase Services)
  • Device identifiers (for analytics/crash reporting)
  • Crash logs and error diagnostics
  • Device model, OS version, RAM & performance states
  • Installation/session identifiers
4.5  Subscription Data (via Google Play Billing)
  • Subscription tier and status
  • Purchase tokens and transaction IDs
  • Subscription start/end dates
  • Platform of purchase (Android)

We do not store credit card numbers or payment instrument details.

4.6  Data We Do Not Collect

Studiely does not collect:

  • Precise GPS location
  • Phone numbers
  • Advertising identifiers for behavioral ads
  • Sensitive identifiers restricted under Google Play Families Policies
  • Biometric identifiers
5
Children & Families Policy Compliance

Studiely may be used by children, including those under the age of 13. We adhere to the following principles:

  • No advertising to children
  • No behavioral tracking
  • Data collection limited to educational use
  • Parental/legal guardian consent required under COPPA for children under 13
  • Parents may review, delete, or correct their child's data
  • Parents may withdraw consent at any time

Parents/legal guardians may submit requests via: admin@qismatventures.com

5.1  COPPA Compliance Measures

To comply with COPPA:

  • Users must enter their date of birth at registration
  • Users under 13 cannot self-create accounts
  • Age validation is performed in real-time
  • Age data is stored securely in Firestore
  • Clear messaging explains parental consent requirements

Under-13 accounts are only permitted with verifiable parental consent.

13
Analytics Controls
13.1  Analytics Opt-Out

Users may opt out of non-essential analytics by:

  • Disabling analytics in device settings, or
  • Contacting admin@qismatventures.com

For users under 13, analytics are disabled by default unless verifiable parental consent is obtained.
