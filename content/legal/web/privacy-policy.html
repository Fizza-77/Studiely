Data We Collect

We collect only data necessary for educational functionality, platform improvement, and legal compliance.

4.1  Account & Identifiers
  • Email address
  • Firebase UID
  • User role (Student, Parent, Teacher, Institution, Administrator)
4.2  Educational Content & Usage Data
  • Study notes, summaries, generated materials
  • Quiz questions, answers, results
  • Curriculum and subject selections
  • Learning progress and performance metrics
  • User preferences and settings
4.3  Analytics Data
  • Page views and navigation patterns
  • Feature usage metrics
  • Approximate location (country/region only)
4.4  Technical & Device Data
  • Browser and device information
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Session identifiers
4.5  Subscription & Payment Data (Web)
  • Subscription tier and status
  • Transaction identifiers
  • Subscription start/end dates
  • Payment platform used (e.g., Paddle)

We do not store credit card or payment instrument details.

4.6  Data We Do Not Collect
  • Precise location data
  • Phone numbers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Biometric identifiers
