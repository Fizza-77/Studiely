Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 14, 2026 Last Updated: February 28, 2026 Platform: Apple App Store

Studiely is an educational technology platform operated by Qismat Ventures W.L.L ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, safety, and rights of all users, including students, parents, teachers, institutions, and administrators, particularly children.

This Privacy Policy applies to users who download and use Studiely via the Apple App Store. It explains what data, if any, your app and any third parties collect from your users, how you and any third parties use the data, and the users' privacy rights. This policy clearly and explicitly identifies what data is collected, how it is collected, and all uses of that data. It also confirms that any third party with whom data is shared (such as service providers) provides the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy. We explain our data retention and deletion policies below, including how a user can revoke consent and/or request deletion of their data.

We collect only the data necessary for providing educational features, improving the app, and ensuring security (data minimization principle). Data is limited to educational purposes and not used for advertising, marketing, or any unauthorized purposes. Paid functionality is not dependent on allowing the app to access data (such as Contacts or Photos). We secure user consent for data collection where required, and users can withdraw consent at any time as described below. If we rely on legitimate interests for processing (e.g., under GDPR), we ensure compliance with applicable laws.

This Privacy Policy is accessible within the app in an easily accessible manner, such as via a dedicated settings or help section.

3
Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required for the app's core educational functionality, account management, analytics to improve the app, and legal compliance. Data collection is explained below, including how it is collected and its uses. We do not collect data for purposes other than those stated without obtaining further consent.

3.1  Account & Identity Data

Collected directly during registration or login via Firebase Authentication.

  • Name (if provided, optional)
  • Email address
  • User role (e.g., Student, Parent, Teacher, Institution, Administrator)
  • Firebase Authentication User ID (UID, a persistent identifier)

Uses: To create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and provide role-based features.

3.2  Educational & Generated Content

Collected when users interact with features, stored via Cloud Firestore.

  • Study notes, summaries, and educational materials generated or inputted
  • Quiz questions, answers, results, scores, and completion data
  • Curriculum selections, subjects, and topics
  • Subject and topic preferences
  • Study modes, difficulty settings, and user preferences
  • Generated content metadata (e.g., subjects, topics, difficulty levels)

Uses: Solely to support learning, track progress, and provide personalized educational assistance. This data is not repurposed without consent.

3.3  Analytics & Usage Data

Collected automatically via Firebase Analytics.

  • Screen views and navigation patterns
  • Feature interactions (e.g., button clicks, feature usage)
  • In-app search history (topics/subjects searched)
  • Quiz performance and completion data
  • Approximate location (country/region only, inferred for service improvement)

We do not collect, use, or share precise location data.

Uses: To analyze app performance, improve user experience, and enhance educational outcomes. Data is anonymized or aggregated where possible. No data is used to build user profiles or for advertising.

3.4  Subscription & Billing Data

Collected via Apple's in-app purchase system.

  • Subscription plan type and status
  • Purchase tokens and transaction identifiers
  • Subscription start and end dates

Payments and subscriptions are processed exclusively by Apple Inc. Studiely does not store or have access to payment card details or other financial information. Apple may collect and process transaction data independently in accordance with its own privacy policy. Subscriptions are managed and can be canceled via your Apple ID settings.

3.5  Device & Technical Data

Collected automatically via Firebase services.

  • Device type, model, and OS version
  • App version
  • Crash logs, diagnostic reports, and error data (e.g., device model, OS version, RAM)
  • Session IDs and installation IDs

Uses: For troubleshooting, crash reporting, and ensuring app compatibility.

3.6  Data We Do Not Collect

Studiely does not collect:

  • Precise location data
  • Phone numbers
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Other sensitive data prohibited under Apple guidelines for children
  • Data from APIs like Contacts, Photos, HealthKit, or Location Services
  • Personal information compiled from device sources or public databases without consent
4
App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

Studiely complies with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. We do not track users or their devices across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising or data brokerage purposes. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any data for third-party advertising, measurement, or sharing with data brokers.

No permission prompt is required via ATT APIs because no such tracking occurs.

5
Children's Privacy – COPPA & Apple Kids Compliance

Studiely may be used by children (including under 13) for educational purposes. We:

  • Comply with COPPA and Apple's guidelines for apps in the Kids Category or handling minors' data
  • Do not serve any behavioral advertising, third-party advertising, or use children's data for marketing
  • Limit data collection to what is strictly necessary for educational functionality and app improvement
  • Require verifiable parental consent before collecting, using, or disclosing personal information from children under 13
  • Allow parents/legal guardians to review, access, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for their child's data at any time
  • Do not include third-party analytics or advertising except in limited, compliant cases
  • Provide a parental gate for any features requiring age verification, distinct from COPPA consent

Our use of Firebase Analytics is anonymized, does not collect/transmit identifiable info like name, DOB, email, precise location, or device info for kids, and is subject to human review for appropriateness.

Parents may request data review, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or manage their child's account by emailing admin@qismatventures.com. We do not send personal information or device info from children to third parties except as necessary for app functionality (e.g., Firebase processing).

Account Deletion

Apps supporting account creation must offer account deletion within the app. You can delete your account and associated data:

  • Directly within the app (via the settings menu or account management section)
  • Or by emailing admin@qismatventures.com and providing your registered email address

We will delete your account and data within 30 days, except where legally required to retain it (e.g., for security, fraud prevention, or accounting purposes). Upon deletion, we revoke all consents and stop processing your data.
